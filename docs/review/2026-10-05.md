## Suggested changes

Nothing here is applied.

1. **`scan.py`: retry Adzuna on 503, fix the JobSpy NaN, and surface partial failures.**
   Evidence: Adzuna NL 503 in 5 of 10 sampled runs and all four countries on 4 Oct;
   `fetch_jobspy` (line 2384) skipped with "cannot convert float NaN to integer" in 4 of
   10. Both are reported as "ok" or "skipped" in the status line and nowhere else, so
   they went unnoticed for at least two weeks. Fix: a retry with backoff on Adzuna, cast
   the NaN-able column with a default in the JobSpy row conversion, and put a warning on
   the dashboard when a source returns less than half its usual raw count. Cost: low;
   the risk is retry time on already long runs.
2. **`scan.py`: suppress re-posts of roles already applied to or hidden.** Evidence: 3 of
   this week's 30 hides (one the same Jobber role, two noted as applied in July) were roles you had already
   applied to or hidden. Match on normalised company plus title against applied and hidden
   rows, not on the listing id, and only for a window (say 120 days). Cost: moderate; a
   genuinely re-opened role after a long gap should come back, hence the window.
3. **`scan.py`: stop surfacing unreadable (thin) rows with junior-looking titles, and
   drop a stated right-to-work requirement.** Evidence: 13 of 30 hides were thin rows
   (Executive, Coordinator, Analyst, Specialist titles) and set-asides doubled to 20.
   The epx 7.0 role carried "right to work" in its JD. Hold thin rows whose title band is
   analyst/specialist/coordinator/executive in the set-aside list instead of the main
   dashboard. Cost: you might miss a good role behind a thin ad; keep them
   available in a collapsed section rather than deleting them.
